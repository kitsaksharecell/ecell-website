About us

Building the founder mindset on campus.

KITS Akshar E-Cell is a student-led entrepreneurship community that helps students understand business, product thinking, marketing, finance, intellectual property, and real-world problem solving.

Our mission is to create a practical startup ecosystem inside college where students can test ideas, collaborate with teams, learn from mentors, and confidently present their innovations.
